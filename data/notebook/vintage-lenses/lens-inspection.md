# Vintage Lens Inspection

Bring a small light and inspect from both ends.

- Fungus: branching or web-like marks
- Haze: a diffuse veil visible across the glass
- Separation: rainbow-edged patches near cemented elements
- Scratches and cleaning marks
- Oily or slow aperture blades
- Smooth, even focusing
- Dents, cross-threading, or damage around the mount and filter ring

> **Collection rule:** A low price is useful only when the lens teaches something, fills a real gap, or can be resold without meaningful loss.
