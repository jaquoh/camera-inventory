# Long-Lens Fieldcraft

## Equivalent framing on the NEX-5N

The physical focal length never changes. The 1.5× crop sensor records a narrower field of view:

| Lens | Full-frame-equivalent framing |
| --- | --- |
| 300mm | about 450mm |
| 400mm | about 600mm |
| 500mm | about 750mm |

## Finding the subject

With a zoom, begin wider, centre the subject, and then zoom in. With a prime, sight along the lens barrel before returning to the viewfinder.

Atmospheric haze and heat shimmer can reduce distant detail even when focus and stability are excellent.
