# Manual Focus

1. Start with a wider view when the lens is a zoom.
2. Place the focus point on a high-contrast edge.
3. Use focus peaking as a guide, not proof.
4. Magnify the view for critical focus when the subject allows it.
5. Brace, exhale gently, and make the exposure.

At long focal lengths, tiny movements shift the framing. Support the lens rather than hanging its weight from the camera mount.
