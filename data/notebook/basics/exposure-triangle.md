# Exposure Triangle

Exposure is controlled by three linked settings:

| Control | Changes light | Also changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aperture | Wider lets in more | Depth of field and rendering |
| Shutter speed | Longer lets in more | Motion blur and camera shake |
| ISO | Raises recorded brightness | Noise and dynamic range |

> **Field note:** Choose the creative constraint first, then balance the other two settings.

With vintage lenses, aperture is usually set on the lens while shutter speed and ISO remain camera controls.
